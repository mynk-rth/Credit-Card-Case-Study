# Credit Card Case Study — Customer Analysis

A short Jupyter-based case study analyzing credit card customers: spend, repayments, and basic customer segments.

## Project Overview
A short Jupyter-based case study analyzing credit card customers, focusing on spend, repayments, and basic segmentation.

Goals:
- Understand customer purchase and repayment behaviour
- Segment customers and identify key segments
- Estimate customer lifetime value (CLV) and churn risk
- Provide simple recommendations to improve retention and revenue
